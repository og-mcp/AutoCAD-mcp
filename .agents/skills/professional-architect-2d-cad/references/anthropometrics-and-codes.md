## 2. Doors, Windows & Threshold Standards

### A. Standard Door Leaf Widths & Heights (Metric)
- **Main Entrance Door**: `1.00 m – 1.20 m` wide × `2.10 m – 2.40 m` high.
- **Habitable Rooms (Living, Bedroom, Study, Kitchen)**: `0.85 m – 0.90 m` wide (`min. 0.80 m`) × `2.10 m` high.
- **Bathrooms, WCs & Utility Rooms**: `0.75 m – 0.80 m` wide (`min. 0.70 m`, or `0.90 m` for accessible ADA bath) × `2.10 m` high.
- **Sliding Patio / Courtyard Glazed Doors**: `1.80 m – 3.60 m` wide (`2, 3, or 4 panels`) × `2.40 m` high.

### B. Window Sizing, Sills & Passive Solar Rules
- **Daylight & Ventilation Code Minimums**:
  - Glazed window area in every habitable room must be **`≥ 10%` (`15%–25%` recommended)** of the room's net floor area.
  - Operable ventilation opening area must be **`≥ 5%`** of the room's net floor area.
- **Standard Sill & Head Heights (above FFL)**:
  - Living / Courtyard Full-Height Glazing: Sill `+0.00 m – +0.15 m`, Head `+2.40 m`.
  - Bedroom / Study Viewing Window: Sill `+0.60 m – +0.90 m`, Head `+2.10 m – +2.40 m`.
  - Kitchen Counter Window: Sill `+1.05 m – +1.10 m` (above `0.90 m` counter + backsplash), Head `+2.10 m – +2.40 m`.
  - Bathroom Clerestory Privacy Window: Sill `+1.50 m – +1.80 m`, Head `+2.10 m – +2.40 m`.

## 3. Staircase Geometry & Ergonomics (Blondel's Law)

Every staircase must satisfy **Blondel's ergonomic stride formula**:
$$600\text{ mm} \le 2R + T \le 630\text{ mm}$$
where:
- $R$ = Riser height (`150 mm – 170 mm` residential; `150 mm` public/commercial; max `175 mm`).
- $T$ = Tread depth (`280 mm – 300 mm` excluding nosing).
- **Exact Riser Calculation**:
  - For a floor-to-floor height $H = 3150\text{ mm}$: choose $N = 20$ risers $\implies R = 3150 / 20 = 157.5\text{ mm}$, $T = 300\text{ mm}$ ($2R + T = 615\text{ mm}$).
- **Landings & Headroom**:
  - Landing length and width must be $\ge$ stair flight width (`min. 1.00 m – 1.20 m`).
  - Maximum vertical rise between landings: `12–16 risers` (`≤ 2.40 m – 2.70 m`).
  - Minimum vertical headroom clearance at every tread nosing: **`2.10 m`**.
